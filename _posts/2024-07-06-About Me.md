I am a predoctoral researcher at the **ARCOS group** (Computer Architecture, Communications and
Systems) at **Universidad Carlos III de Madrid**, working with David Expósito Singh on large-scale
epidemiological simulation in collaboration with Spain's *Centro Nacional de Epidemiología*. In
parallel I am completing the MSc in Computer Science and Technology at UC3M.

My work lives where high-performance computing meets data that comes from the real world and is
therefore messy: parallel and distributed storage, MPI/OpenMP, simulation, and time-series models
over sensor and surveillance data.

Before this I spent a year and a half at **Geotab** writing production firmware for connected
telematics devices — NuttX RTOS and embedded Linux, C/C++, test-driven development and
hardware-in-the-loop validation, in a team split between Spain and Canada. I have not left that
world behind: real-time and embedded systems are part of the research angle I am building.

## Research interests

High-performance computing · parallel and distributed storage · real-time and dependable systems ·
simulation · time-series machine learning
